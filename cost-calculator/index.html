Why ECS Fargate over EC2:
Raw EC2 scaling takes 20–40 minutes of total downtime (unmounting EBS, changing instance size, re-attaching IPs). In contrast, ECS Fargate auto-scales horizontally in 20–30 seconds with 0 downtime during exam rushes and pays only for exact seconds consumed.
🛡️
Why RDS Proxy is Mandatory:
When 100+ students submit papers simultaneously, serverless Lambda workers spin up concurrently. Direct connections crash PostgreSQL with FATAL: max_connections exceeded. RDS Proxy multiplexes them safely into 15–25 stable connections.
💡
1-AZ Cost Optimization ($278/mo):
Pairing 1-AZ NAT Gateway with Single-AZ RDS drops the monthly cloud bill from $406 to $278 (saving ~$128/mo). Automated daily snapshots provide robust disaster recovery for early-stage rollout.

  • Compare diagram formats: SVG and raster requests have different costs and quality tradeoffs; use the controls to compare estimates.
