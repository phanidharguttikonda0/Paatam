# AWS SES Sandbox Exit & Production Moving Guide

When an AWS account is newly created or starts using Amazon Simple Email Service (SES), it is placed in the **SES Sandbox environment by default** (in region `ap-south-2` or whichever region is active).

---

## 1. What Are the SES Sandbox Limitations?

While in the Sandbox:
- **Recipient Restrictions**: You can **ONLY** send emails to:
  - Verified email addresses or domains in SES (e.g., any address `@paatam.in` since domain `paatam.in` is verified).
  - Attempting to send to any external address (e.g., `user@gmail.com`, `someone@outlook.com`) fails immediately with `500 Internal Server Error` / `MessageRejected: Email address is not verified`.
- **Sending Quota**:
  - Maximum of **200 emails per 24-hour period**.
  - Maximum sending rate of **1 email per second**.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Instructions to Request Production Access

To send emails to external end-users and students/admins across all email providers, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Open the SES Console
1. Log in to the [AWS Management Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/).
2. Select your deployment region: **Asia Pacific (Hyderabad) `ap-south-2`**.
3. Search for and navigate to **Amazon Simple Email Service**.

### Step 2: Request Production Access
1. In the left navigation menu, click **Account dashboard**.
2. In the banner titled **"Account status: Sandbox"**, click the button **Request production access** (or **Edit account details**).

### Step 3: Fill Out the Production Access Form
AWS Support reviews this form to prevent spam. Complete the fields with the following details:

- **Mail Type**:
  - Select **Transactional** (Account verification OTPs, password resets, system alerts, student assignment notices).
- **Website URL**:
  - `https://paatam.in` (Ensure the landing page or portal is accessible).
- **Use Case Description**:
  - Provide a clear, professional explanation. Template below:
  > *"Paatam is an educational learning and school management platform. Emails sent via SES are strictly transactional, including: one-time registration and login verification OTPs (via AWS SES), account setup invitations for school administrators and teachers, and academic notifications. All recipients are registered users who explicitly initiated the authentication or invitation workflow. We do not send marketing or bulk promotional emails. Bounces and complaints are monitored via Amazon SNS and automatically suppressed to maintain 0% bounce rate. Expected initial volume: 1,000 - 5,000 transactional emails per day."*
- **Recipient Opt-in / Acquisition**:
  - Confirm that recipients explicitly initiate requests (e.g. entering their email on the login/signup screen or being invited by their school administrator).
- **Bounce & Complaint Handling**:
  - Confirm that bounce and complaint notifications are linked to Amazon SNS topics (`paatam-ses-notifications-dev` / `prod`) and monitored.
- **Region**:
  - Confirm the region is `ap-south-2`.

### Step 4: Submit and Await Review
1. Click **Submit request**.
2. AWS Trust & Safety team typically reviews and approves requests within **24 hours**.
3. Once approved, your status in the Account Dashboard changes from **Sandbox** to **Production**, and your sending rate and 24-hour quota will automatically increase.

## 3. Pre-Requisite DNS Verification Checklist

Before or during production approval, ensure all domain authentications are configured in your DNS provider (Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Route53, Hostinger):

1. **DKIM Tokens**:
   - Ensure the 3 CNAME records generated by Terraform (`aws_ses_domain_dkim.main.dkim_tokens`) are added to your DNS.
2. **SPF Record**:
   - Add or update the TXT record on `paatam.in`:
     ```text
     v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all
     ```
3. **DMARC Record**:
   - Add a TXT record for `_dmarc.paatam.in`:
     ```text
     v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:postmaster@paatam.in
     ```
   *(Protects domain reputation and prevents emails from landing in spam folders of Gmail/Outlook).*

---

## 4. Environment Variables Checklist for Production

When switching ECS `NODE_ENV` to `production`:
1. In `src/controllers/auth.controller.ts`:
   - Verify `process.env.NODE_ENV === 'production'` is active so OTPs are **never** returned in the HTTP response body.
2. Verify SES credentials and verified sender email (`MAIL_FROM=no-reply@paatam.in`).
